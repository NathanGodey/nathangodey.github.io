I'm very happy to share that my PhD thesis, [*Improving Representations for Language Modeling*](/publications/#thesis), was awarded the **2025 [ATALA](https://www.atala.org/) best PhD thesis prize** at the TALN conference (Marseille, July 2025).

<img src="/img/posts/atala_prize.jpeg" alt="Receiving the ATALA prize certificate at TALN 2025" style="max-width:500px;"/>

The full citation from the jury (in French) is available on the [ATALA website](https://www.atala.org/). A short excerpt, translated:

> Nathan Godey's thesis sets out to go beyond the structural limitations of representation learning in neural architectures. Through detailed analysis using interpretability methods, he proposes a strong conceptual contribution: a method for training language models that does away with computing the distribution over output symbols, by leveraging contrastive learning techniques. This original approach, called *headless*, aims to reconstruct sequences of input token embeddings without resorting to a decoding head during training. A demanding, systematic, and technically accomplished experimental study demonstrated the effectiveness of this new paradigm, both in terms of memory usage and convergence speed.

The prize was awarded jointly with [Urchade Zaratiana](https://urchade.github.io/) (LIPN, Université Sorbonne Paris Nord, CNRS). Congrats to him as well!
