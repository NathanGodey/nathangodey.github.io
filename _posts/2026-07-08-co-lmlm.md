**Abstract**<br/>
Externalizing knowledge in LLM pre-training is a promising avenue to achieve higher performance at smaller scales, control knowledge use, and overall increase model transparency. We propose continuous-query limited memory language models (Co-LMLM), an LLM that interleaves flexible vector retrieval queries with next-token predictions, and is pre-trained to copy knowledge returned from the KB, rather than memorize it. Co-LMLM is pre-trained with a scalable approach that jointly trains a knowledge-externalizing LLM, induces its knowledge base, and learns an expressive continuous retrieval mechanism. Across pre-training at multiple model scales, Co-LMLM outperforms prior knowledge-externalizing and vanilla LLMs in both perplexity and factual precision. At 360M scale, this includes lower perplexity than models pre-trained on 40× more data, and SimpleQA-verified performance that is in line with gpt-4o-mini and higher than Claude Sonnet 4.5.

This paper was led by Yair Feldman and co-authored with Linxi Zhao, Dongyoung Go, Yilun Hua, Kilian Q. Weinberger, Jennifer J. Sun, and my postdoctoral advisor [Yoav Artzi](https://yoavartzi.com/) at Cornell.
